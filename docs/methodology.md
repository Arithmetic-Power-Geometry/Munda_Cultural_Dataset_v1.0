# Methodology

1. Mine historical, governmental, academic and community sources into source-level claims.
2. Map claims to the hierarchical ontology and, where verified, HRAF OCM categories.
3. Treat literature-derived claims as candidates until contemporary field verification.
4. Capture interviews at segment level; observations at event/step level; objects and media as stable entities.
5. Link every interpretation to evidence IDs.
6. Preserve contradictions and variants rather than forcing consensus.
7. Validate terminology and interpretation with participating community reviewers.
8. Derive commonality statistics only from assessed sites/participants/events, never from missing data.
9. Export public, controlled and restricted releases separately.
